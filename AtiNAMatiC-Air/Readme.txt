AtiNAMatiC Air

AtiNAMatiC-Air is a post-cab / post-IR "amp-in-the-room" feel enhancer. It adds the acoustic qualities that make a guitar sound like it's coming from a real speaker in a real room rather than a flat IR capture:

Stage	What it does
Air shelf (~10 kHz)	0–+5 dB presence boost — the "breathy" top-end feel
Presence peak (~5 kHz)	Gentle +2 dB — forward mid-high definition
M/S sides gain	Subtle stereo widening (×1.0–1.6)
Studio mode	Tight/controlled; no low-mid colouration
Room mode	Bigger air + ~80 Hz speaker resonance peak + side Haas delay for depth
Mix & Output	Wet/dry blend + output trim
Signal chain position: place it last — after your amp sim (NAM, ToneX, etc.) and after your cab/IR loader:

Guitar → Amp Sim → Cab/IR → [AtiNAMatiC-Air] → DAW
