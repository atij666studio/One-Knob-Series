AtiNAMatiC Bloom

Held notes swell and sing, like a loud amp blooming. Bloom remembers each
pick attack and brings the decaying sustain back up toward it (never above),
with a touch of harmonic richness. A new attack resets it instantly.

Signal chain position: before the amp sim.

Guitar -> [AtiNAMatiC-Bloom] -> Amp Sim -> Cab/IR -> DAW

BLOOM   how much of the sustain is brought back (0 = untouched)
TIME    Swell / Sustain / Singing
MIX     blend with the dry signal
OUTPUT  final trim

See docs/AtiNAMatiC-Bloom-Manual.md for details.
