AtiNAMatiC Cohere

Tames amp-sim harshness and glues the guitar together. The harsh 3-4 kHz bite
and 6-9 kHz fizz are cut only while they stick out above the guitar's own
normal balance, then a gentle compressor and a touch of tape saturation glue
the result. Level stays put.

Signal chain position: after the amp sim and cab/IR.

Guitar -> Amp Sim -> Cab/IR -> [AtiNAMatiC-Cohere] -> DAW

COHERE     taming + glue + saturation (0 = untouched)
CHARACTER  Smooth / Glue / Tape
MIX        parallel blend
OUTPUT     final trim

See docs/AtiNAMatiC-Cohere-Manual.md for details.
