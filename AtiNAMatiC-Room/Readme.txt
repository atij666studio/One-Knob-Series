AtiNAMatiC Room

A short, dense room reverb that ducks while you play and blooms in the gaps,
so dense amp-sim mixes stay clear. Low end of the reverb is filtered out, the
dry signal is never touched, stereo room even from a mono guitar.

Signal chain position: after the amp sim and cab/IR, as an insert.

Guitar -> Amp Sim -> Cab/IR -> [AtiNAMatiC-Room] -> DAW

ROOM    how much room you hear (0 = dry)
SPACE   Booth / Studio / Hall
DUCK    how far the reverb dips while you play
OUTPUT  final trim

See docs/AtiNAMatiC-Room-Manual.md for details.
