AtiNAMatiC Choke

Shortens how long notes ring after you play them. Once a note has fallen a
little below its own peak, Choke makes the rest of the decay fall faster, so
stops are tighter. The pick attack is never touched; every new attack
restores full level.

Signal chain position: before the amp (tighter playing) or after the
amp/cab (shorter amp ring-out).

Guitar -> [AtiNAMatiC-Choke] -> Amp Sim -> Cab/IR -> DAW

CHOKE   how fast the decay is pulled down (0 = untouched)
STYLE   Tight / Staccato / Djent
MIX     blend with the dry signal
OUTPUT  final trim

See docs/AtiNAMatiC-Choke-Manual.md for details.
