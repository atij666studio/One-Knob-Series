## ONE KNOB Series v1.0.0

23 one-knob plug-ins for guitar and mixing, by ATIJ STUDIO. **Free and open source (GPL v3)**, no account or sign-up.

### Pick what you want

| System | All-in-one installer (tick the plug-ins and formats you want) | Just one plug-in |
|---|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 (64-bit) | `ONE-KNOB-Series-v1.0.0-Windows-x64-Setup.exe` | `AtiNAMatiC-<Name>-v1.0.0-Windows-x64.zip` |
| macOS 10.15+ (Apple Silicon and Intel) | `ONE-KNOB-Series-v1.0.0-macOS-Installer.pkg` (click **Customize**) | `AtiNAMatiC-<Name>-v1.0.0-macOS.zip` |
| Linux x86_64 | `ONE-KNOB-Series-v1.0.0-Linux-x86_64.tar.gz` (run `./install.sh`) | `AtiNAMatiC-<Name>-v1.0.0-Linux-x86_64.zip` |
| Linux ARM64 (e.g. Raspberry Pi 5) | `ONE-KNOB-Series-v1.0.0-Linux-ARM64.tar.gz` (run `./install.sh`) | `AtiNAMatiC-<Name>-v1.0.0-Linux-ARM64.zip` |

**Formats:** VST3, CLAP and LV2 on every system, plus AU on macOS. Each zip contains every format for that plug-in and its manual. The full manual for all 23 is `ONE-KNOB-Series-v1.0.0-Manual.pdf`.

**macOS:** the plug-ins aren't signed by Apple. If macOS blocks the installer, right-click it and choose **Open**. If you copy files from a zip, run `xattr -cr` on them (see the manual).

### What's new in v1.0.0

- First public release, now free and open source.
- New look across the whole series, and 7 new plug-ins: Bloom, Cab, Choke, Hum, Mono, Pitch and Slow.
- Every plug-in format: VST3, CLAP and LV2 on Windows, macOS and Linux, plus AU on macOS. The standalone apps have been retired; these work best inside your DAW.
- New installers that let you choose exactly which plug-ins and formats to install.
- Updated manuals with screenshots.

Built with JUCE and clap-juce-extensions.
