AtiNAMatiC Strum

Three-band strum shaper. Lifts the string "strike" in the highs, adds a little
ring in the mids and pulls the boom/thump of the strum down in the lows, so
chords sound articulate without getting boomier or louder.

Signal chain position: before the amp sim (electric), or anywhere on a clean /
acoustic track.

Guitar -> [AtiNAMatiC-Strum] -> Amp Sim -> Cab/IR -> DAW

STRUM    how strongly the bands are shaped (0 = untouched, 100 = polished)
VOICING  Clean / Crunch / Acoustic
MIX      blend with the dry signal
OUTPUT   final trim

See docs/AtiNAMatiC-Strum-Manual.md for details.
