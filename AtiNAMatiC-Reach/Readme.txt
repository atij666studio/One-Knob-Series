AtiNAMatiC Reach

Makes the guitar step forward and spread out without getting louder, muddier
or less mono-safe: presence lift and mud dip in the centre, low end folded to
mono, highs widened, and a mono-safe spread generated for mono amp sims.

Signal chain position: after the amp sim and cab/IR, on a stereo output.

Guitar -> Amp Sim -> Cab/IR -> [AtiNAMatiC-Reach] -> DAW

REACH   projection + spread (0 = untouched)
SHAPE   Focus / Wide / Huge
MIX     blend with the dry signal
OUTPUT  final trim

See docs/AtiNAMatiC-Reach-Manual.md for details.
