AtiNAMatiC Mono

Stereo where it helps, mono where it matters. Bass makes the low end mono
below a crossover you set; Full narrows the whole image to mono; Safe keeps
the lows mono and holds the loudness as it narrows. Flags phase problems.

Signal chain position: anywhere after something that makes stereo.

Amp Sim -> Double / Reach / Air -> [AtiNAMatiC-Mono] -> DAW

MONO    crossover (Bass) or narrowing amount (Full / Safe)
MODE    Bass / Full / Safe
OUTPUT  final trim

See docs/AtiNAMatiC-Mono-Manual.md for details.
